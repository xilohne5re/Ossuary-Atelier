What people say

Questions

Where is Ossuary Atelier based?

Phuket, Thailand. Every piece is sourced locally, from shops, weekend markets and secondhand stalls across the island.

How do I buy something?

There's no cart. Browse the shop, then DM @ossuaryatelier on Instagram to claim a piece. We confirm availability and arrange handover or shipping in the chat.

What does “provenance” mean here?

For select pieces we publish a written story page — where it was sourced, its era and material, and why it was worth keeping. It's a documented record, not a claim or a certificate.

Is the Phuket Thrift Guide really free?

Yes. The guide, the map and the tools are free and ad-free, with no tracking cookies and no personal data collection. If you want to keep them that way, see Support.

Do you use AI?

Sparingly, and honestly. We're not anti-technology — we're exploring how to use new tools for creative, human work without letting them replace the writing, sourcing and taste that make this worth doing. Read more on the About page.
