How to Thrift in Phuket

What to expect

Most of Phuket's best secondhand stock is Japanese imports — reliable quality, unusual cuts, and prices that make no sense until you remember shipping is $5. Phuket Town is the densest hunting ground: most of the second hand shops below sit inside the town bowl, close enough to walk between. Further north, near Cherngtalay and the Sarasin Bridge, are the local low-price shops that most visitors never reach.

When to go

Weekday mornings are the quieter hours at the densest shops — the same stock, half the crowd. Weekend markets like Phuket Chatuchak run for days at a stretch, but weekday mornings still tend to hold the best picks before the stalls get picked over.

What to look for

  • Japanese domestic-market labels
  • Heavyweight knits and jacquards
  • Deadstock with tags intact
  • Shoes worth rehabbing — dedicated secondhand shoe shops sit across the island
