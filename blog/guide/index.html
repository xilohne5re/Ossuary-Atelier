How to Thrift in Phuket

What to expect

Most of Phuket's best secondhand stock is Japanese imports — reliable quality, unusual cuts, and prices that make no sense until you remember shipping is $5. (Copy to be written.)

When to go

Weekday mornings are the quieter hours at the densest shops — the same stock, half the crowd. (Copy to be written.)

What to look for

  • Japanese domestic-market labels
  • Heavyweight knits and jacquards
  • Deadstock with tags intact
