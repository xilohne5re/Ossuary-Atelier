The Guide

Phuket's living thrift guide — real shop reviews, sourcing notes, an open map, and the tools the archive actually uses.

We are Ossuary Atelier — a secondhand studio in Phuket that recovers garments and keeps the receipts on everything we find. This hub is the front door to everything we publish: the Phuket thrift directory, sourcing notes and field reports, an open map of the island's markets, beaches, and activities, and small tools we build along the way. Questions, answered

Phuket Town holds the densest concentration of secondhand shops. The thrift directory covers each shop with real reviews, price ranges, and sourcing notes.

Mostly — everyday basics can run a few hundred baht, while curated Japanese imports climb higher. Each shop entry lists its price range.

Japanese domestic-market labels, heavyweight knits and jacquards, and deadstock with tags intact. Slow digging wins.

Weekday mornings — freshly stocked racks, no crowd, better negotiating light.
