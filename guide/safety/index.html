Critical: Box jellyfish — rinse with vinegar (4–6%) liberally for ≥30 s, remove tentacles with tweezers (not bare fingers), seek medical care for severe reactions. Do NOT use fresh water, urine, alcohol or rub. Bluebottles / Physalia — hot water at ~45 °C for ~20 min is the evidence-based approach; vinegar is not indicated. For elapid neurotoxic bites use pressure-immobilisation; for most viper bites immobilise at heart level and get to hospital/antivenom. For unknown mushrooms with delayed symptoms or onset >5 h — go to hospital ASAP (call 1367 / 1422).

Sea & Shore

Phuket's west coast sees seasonal box jellyfish presence in places; postings change year to year. Unposted does not mean safe. Follow red and purple advisories, speak to a lifeguard if present, and keep small children in shallow, guarded areas. The west coast (Kata, Karon, Patong, Surin, Nai Harn) takes the full southwest monsoon swell from May to October, which is when rip currents run hardest.

License: CC BY 2.0. First-aid differs from box jelly — hot water ~45 °C for ~20 min, no vinegar.

  • Rip currents: if caught, don't fight straight back — swim parallel to shore until out of the narrow current, then angle in. Float and wave for help if exhausted.
  • Venomous fish: stonefish are well-camouflaged in reef and shallow rock; shuffle feet in murky shallows where advised.
  • Sea snakes: usually not aggressive, but never handle them.
  • Blue-ringed octopus: small, reclusive, deadly neurotoxin — do not touch.

Undergrowth

Most antivenom-treated snakebites in Thailand are Malayan pit vipers. Watch where you put hands and feet in leaf litter, especially at dusk. Macaques are wild — do not feed, do not snatch food, and secure bags. Monkey bites must be taken seriously (rabies and B-virus): wash with soap and water for 15 minutes and seek PEP assessment immediately.

  • Snakes: stay still if unsure, back away slowly, don't try to kill or handle.
  • Elephants: give a very wide berth on trails and roads; never approach a cow with a calf.
  • Slow loris: protected and venomous — illegal to touch or photograph for money.
  • Scorpions and leeches: check boots and gear after wet scrub.

Green & Poison

Rule 1: if you didn't buy it from a known edible source, don't eat wild mushrooms or unknown plants. Folk tests (silver spoon, boiling with onion) are not reliable. Some deadly species look like edible ones. If ingestion is suspected or symptoms are delayed (>5 h) — go to hospital now. Keep a sample or photo and note the time eaten if safe to do so.

  • Poison Control: Ramathibodi 1367
  • DDC (Department of Disease Control): 1422
  • Sea mango (Cerbera odollam): extremely toxic — never chew or eat.
  • Dumb cane (Dieffenbachia): irritant and caustic — keep away from children.

Islands & Body

Most drowning deaths are rip currents. Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real. Phuket has experienced landslides after heavy rain — avoid steep, freshly saturated slopes. Tsunami evacuation routes and towers exist; pay attention to warnings. Dengue is year-round — use repellent, cover up at dawn and dusk, and avoid aspirin and ibuprofen if febrile until dengue is ruled out.

Public Domain (CDC). Dengue is year-round in Phuket — use repellent, cover up at dawn and dusk, and avoid aspirin or ibuprofen if febrile until dengue is ruled out.

  • Heat: hydrate, avoid midday exertion, watch for confusion and dry skin (heat stroke is an emergency).
  • Floods and landslides: never cross flooded roads or rivers; avoid slopes during and after intense rain.
  • Dengue, leptospirosis, melioidosis: prevention is key — cover, repel, and avoid wading in floodwater with open cuts where possible.

Field Protocols

When in doubt, call 1669 and go to hospital. This is harm-reduction, not a substitute for professional care. Ossuary Atelier is not medical staff.

License: CC BY-SA 4.0. Always follow the flag posted on the day — systems vary slightly between beaches.

  • Marine envenomation: follow the correct first-aid for the specific organism (vinegar vs hot water). Don't delay transfer for severe symptoms.
  • Snakebite: keep calm and still, immobilise the limb, remove tight jewellery, don't cut, suck or tourniquet.
  • Animal bite or scratch: wash soap and water for 15 minutes, cover, seek PEP assessment the same day.
  • Suspected poisoning: call 1367 or 1422, don't induce vomiting blindly, go to the ER.

Quick FAQ — Phuket Beach Safety

  • When is jellyfish season in Phuket? May to October (southwest monsoon), especially July to September. Check DMCR marine warnings.
  • Box jellyfish sting? Vinegar (4 to 6 percent) for 30 seconds or more, remove tentacles with tweezers. Don't use fresh water, urine, alcohol or rub. Call 1669 if severe.
  • Rip current at Kata, Karon or Patong? Don't swim straight back — swim parallel to shore until out, then angle in. Float and wave for help if exhausted.
  • Monkey bite? Wash with soap and water for 15 minutes, seek PEP assessment the same day. Don't feed macaques.
  • Emergency numbers? 1669 EMS, 1155 Tourist Police (EN), 1196 Marine/Diving, 1367 Poison Control.
