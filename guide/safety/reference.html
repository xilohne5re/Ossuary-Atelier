First-Aid Do & Don't Matrix

SituationDoDon't
Box jellyfish (cubozoan)Vinegar 4–6% liberally ≥30 s; remove tentacles with tweezers; call 1669 if severeFresh water, urine, alcohol, rub, scrape with fingers
Bluebottle / PhysaliaHot water immersion ~45 °C for ~20 min; pain management; seek care if severeVinegar (not indicated); fresh water may aggravate in some cases
Stonefish / venomous fishImmobilise, hot water as directed for pain, get to hospital/antivenom per protocolWalk on reef blindly; delay transfer for severe pain/systemic symptoms
Elapid bite (neurotoxic)Pressure-immobilisation per current guidance, keep calm/still, call 1669/go to ERCut/suck, tourniquet, exercise limb
Viper bite (haemotoxic)Immobilise at heart level, remove tight jewellery, keep calm, go to hospital for antivenomCut/suck, tight arterial tourniquet, alcohol
Macaque/scratch/biteWash with soap/water ≥15 min, cover, seek PEP assessment same day (1155/1669)Ignore; delay; "let it dry"
Unknown mushroom / suspected poisoningKeep sample/photo/time, call 1367 or 1422, go to ER ASAP if delayed onset (>5 h)Folk tests (silver spoon/onion), induce vomiting blindly
Rip currentDon't fight straight back; swim parallel to shore until out, then angle in; float/wave for helpPanic and swim against current
Febrile (possible dengue)Hydrate, rest, seek care; avoid aspirin/ibuprofen until dengue ruled outSelf-medicate with NSAIDs blindly

Beach Flags (Summary)

  • Red — dangerous conditions, usually no swimming / strong currents
  • Yellow — caution, moderate hazard
  • Red/Yellow — supervised area / conditions vary
  • Purple — marine pests present (e.g. jellyfish/stings) — heed advice
  • Double red / posted closure — do not enter water

Flags vary slightly by beach/lifeguard; always follow the posted flag and local advice on the day.

Hospital / Antivenom / Hyperbaric (Phuket Context)

  • Bangkok Hospital Phuket — +66 76 254 425 (has hyperbaric/recompression services in general; confirm availability at the time)
  • SSS Hyperbaric Services Thailand — 081-0819000
  • Antivenom: provided by facilities per national supply; for snakebites go to the nearest appropriate ER — don't delay.
  • Diving emergency: call 1196 and follow hospital instructions.

These are starting points; numbers/services change over time — call and confirm if unsure.

Quick Quiz (15)

Box jellyfish sting: first step?

Vinegar for many cubozoans; remove tentacles with tweezers.

Bluebottle (Physalia): correct?

Different from box jelly — hot water is supported.

Caught in a rip current?

Don't fight the current.

Macaque bite/scratch?

Rabies/B-virus — wash immediately and get assessed.

Unknown mushroom, delayed symptoms (>5 h)?

Amatoxin poisoning can be delayed.

Elapid (neurotoxic) snakebite?

Don't cut/suck/tourniquet.

Viper bite?

Most viper bites don't need arterial tourniquets.

Febrile and possible dengue?

Bleeding risk with NSAIDs in some dengue cases.

Slow loris?

Protected and can be venomous.

Floodwater with open cut?

Leptospirosis/melioidosis risk increases.

Marine warning purple/red?

Check DMCR warnings.

Life-threatening emergency?

Call EMS early.

Diving emergency?

Marine/diving emergency line.

Poison control?

1367 / 1422 for food/mushrooms.

Stonefish sting?

Severe pain — don't delay transfer.
