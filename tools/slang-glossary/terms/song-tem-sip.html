song tem sip2/10

hear the word
also 2/10 General slang

Meaning

a deliberately low score for something secretly too good to share — gatekeeping as criticism

Literally: two out of ten

In a sentence

ร้านนี้ 2/10 ห้ามไปนะ

rahn ni song tem sip, ham pai na

This place is a 2/10 — don't go (it's actually amazing).

How it’s used

deep internet vernacular, credited to Gen Alpha

The scores

usefulness

slanginess

Usefulness runs from “rarely needed, mostly colour” to “essential, daily use”. Slanginess runs from a plain standard word up to deep-cut insider slang.
