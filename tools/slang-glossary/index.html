How the two scores work

Usefulness

How often you will actually need this word — from a greeting you use all day down to a term that is mostly colour. Practical vocabulary scores high.

Slanginess

How far the word sits from standard or formal Thai. Low means a plain everyday word; high means deep-cut insider slang that only people in the know use.
