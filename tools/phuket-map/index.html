About this map of second hand shops in Phuket

Every pin on this map is a documented visit from the archive's own walks — real second hand shops, thrift stores, and vintage dealers across Phuket, from the Phuket Town bowl up to Cherngtalay and the north of the island. Where we've written a full review, the pin links straight to it: what to look for, what it costs, and when to go. The map is free to use and the data behind it is open — reuse it, embed it, or plan your own secondhand crawl around it. We keep adding pins as we visit more of the island.
